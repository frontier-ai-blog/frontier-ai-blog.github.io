About us

A research and engineering team inside Microsoft Office.

Frontier AI is part of Microsoft Office. We are researchers, engineers, and product people who work on Copilot. Our focus is the knowledge work that is still hard for AI: tasks that span several apps, depend on context built up over weeks, and need to be right the first time.

We run controlled experiments, build prototypes, and turn the results into design principles for agents. This site is where we publish that work.

Our story

We study AI where knowledge work already happens.

Much of the world's knowledge work runs through Office documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and email. Our team sits inside Microsoft Office, close to that work, and we work on Copilot so it can take on more of it.

Building agents for real work raised questions that public benchmarks rarely cover: how much an agent should remember about a person or a project, which decisions belong to the model and which belong to deterministic code, and when a smaller, faster model is the better choice. Those questions shape most of our research.

We started this blog in 2026 to share that research in the open. Each post is written by someone on the team and covers an experiment, a design principle, or a result we think other builders can use.
