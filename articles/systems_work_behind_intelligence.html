The Systems Work Behind Useful Intelligence

What dependable agent experiences demand from data, infrastructure, evaluation, and operations.

Frontier AI for Office Science / Aug 14, 2026

Sample content: This placeholder demonstrates the article template and can be replaced when a published draft is ready.

Capability is only the visible layer

A useful agent experience rests on systems that make model behavior observable, repeatable, and recoverable. The model may produce the visible answer, but the surrounding architecture determines whether that answer arrives with the right context and evidence.

Data preparation, tool contracts, identity, policy, and telemetry are product behavior. Treating them as background infrastructure makes failures harder to diagnose and improvements harder to measure.

Design for the full trajectory

Teams need to evaluate more than final output quality. A dependable system also makes sound intermediate choices, handles unavailable tools, preserves user intent, and exposes uncertainty at the right time.

  • Record the decisions that shaped each trajectory.
  • Make retries and fallbacks explicit.
  • Measure latency and cost alongside task success.
